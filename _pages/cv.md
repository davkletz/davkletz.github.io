## Research

Since 2026 at LaTTiCe, I work on multilingual models (their representations and tokenization) and on latent communication between agents.

During my postdoc at IDSIA (2025 to 2026), I worked on hallucination detection, multilinguality, and LLM applications in finance.

My PhD (2021 to 2025) studied how BERT and RoBERTa encode negation: its syntactic scope, its compositional meaning, and veridicality.
